Key Details

  • Breadcrumb: Home / Section / Story Title — Home and Section are links, title is plain text (truncated if long)
  • Section tag in the story header also becomes a link to the section page
  • Reading time appears in the meta line next to author and date
  • "More from" shows up to 3 stories from the same section, excluding the current story
  • If the section has fewer than 2 other stories, the block is hidden
  • "All Technology Stories →" links to the section page
  • The 3-column grid collapses to 2 on tablet, 1 on mobile
